What EduSphere does

The everyday work of a school, in one system.

Each part is switched on only if your institution needs it. Nothing is hidden behind jargon.

Student records and admissions

Take applications, review them, issue offers and keep one clean student file from admission to graduation.

Fees and receipts with balances

Build fee structures, generate invoices, receive payments and see every student's balance and receipt.

Results and report cards

Enter marks, moderate and release them, then print branded report cards and transcripts.

Attendance and timetables

Mark registers in seconds, spot absences, and keep class and teacher timetables in step.

Parent portal

Parents switch between their children, see results, attendance and fees, and pay online.

Lecturer and teacher tools

Course sites, assignments, quizzes, gradebooks and mark entry in the same place as the timetable.

Library, boarding and transport

Loans and fines, hostel rolls and exeats, bus routes and pick-up lists — without separate spreadsheets.

Reports and dashboards

Collections, debtors, enrolment, attendance and at-risk students in plain charts a head can act on.

Admin, HR and compliance

Leave, payroll, procurement, estates, policies and governance records round out the office.

How it works

Start small, then grow into every department.

The setup wizard creates your year, classes, roles and first administrator. You can switch modules on as you need them.

  1. Set up your institution

    Name, branding, academic year, terms, currency and the modules you use.

  2. Add students and staff

    Import a spreadsheet or add people one at a time. Roles decide what each person can see.

  3. Collect fees and record results

    Issue invoices, receive payments, enter marks and release results when they are ready.

  4. Keep parents informed

    Parents sign in to see results, attendance, balances and notices — and to pay online.

Secure and reliable

Records are private by default.

EduSphere is built for one institution at a time. Permission checks run on every screen and every API call, and sensitive views are logged.

  • Data protectionTenant isolation, restricted registers and consent-aware sharing.
  • Audit trailMoney, marks, users and roles leave a dated record of who did what.
