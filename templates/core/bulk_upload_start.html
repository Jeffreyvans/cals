What this upload can do

  • Find existing students by registration number, email or username, and update only the columns you supply.
  • Flag a surname, date of birth and programme match for review; it never merges records automatically.
  • Add missing programmes, courses, classes, years and enrolments, but only when you tick those options.
  • Show every create, update, skip and error on the preview screen before anything is saved.
